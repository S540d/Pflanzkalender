# Pflanzkalender

Plan your garden year at a glance: a planting calendar that shows when to sow, plant, care for and harvest — with 32 ready-made plants.

## Open the app

- Web: [s540d.github.io/Pflanzkalender](https://s540d.github.io/Pflanzkalender/)
- Android: [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.github.s540d.pflanzkalender&referrer=utm_source%3Dgithub_readme%26utm_medium%3Dreferral%26utm_campaign%3Dweb_presence)

## What you can do

- **See the whole year** in half-month steps; the current period is highlighted.
- **Agenda:** what is due now and in the next five half-months.
- **32 plants included** (vegetables, flowers, trees) with typical activities, sun/shade needs and climate tips for hot, dry spots.
- **Make it yours:** add your own plants, edit or delete activities, shift them earlier/later or drag them to a new period.
- **Filter and search** by vegetables, flowers or trees.
- **Templates:** start from a community template, or export, import and share your plan (file or QR code).
- Light/dark mode, 8 languages, works offline.

## Privacy

Your data stays on your device. No login, no personal data collected.
